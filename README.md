# Quantum-Computing-Learning 
An advanced implementation of quantum algorithms and robotic kinematics matrices based on MIT curriculum standards (MIT 8.371 / MIT 6.832).

## 🔬 Core Mathematical Foundation
- **Day 2 (Quantum):** 2-Qubit Bell State Maximal Entanglement Vector.
- **Day 4 (Quantum):** N-Qubit GHZ Statevector Tensor Product Expansion:
  $$\vert\text{GHZ}\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\vert0\rangle^{\otimes N} + \vert1\rangle^{\otimes N})$$
- **Day 5-7 (Robotics):** 4x4 Homogeneous Transformation & Nonlinear Analytical Inverse Kinematics (IK) closed-loop solver:
  $$T = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta & 0 & x \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta & 0 & y \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & z \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$
- **Day 8-9 (Quantum):** QAOA ansatz compilation and VQE parameter optimization loop.
